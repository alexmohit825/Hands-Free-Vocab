About the App

Hands-Free Vocab is an eyes-free, voice-controlled English vocabulary learning application designed specifically for commuters, drivers, and hands-busy learners. Master 1,800 high-yield words across 60 progressive sets without ever touching or glancing at your phone screen.

Voice Command Quick Reference

While driving or studying hands-free, simply speak naturally into your vehicle's microphone or Bluetooth audio:

Voice Command Action
"Next" / "Skip" Immediately advances to the next vocabulary card.
"Repeat" / "Again" Re-plays the acoustic phonetic pronunciation and hook of the current word.
"Mastered" / "Got It" Marks the word as mastered, records spaced repetition metrics, and advances.
"Explain" / "Detail" Speaks the full comprehensive lexical definition and nuances.
"Example" / "Sentence" Narrates the real-world contextual usage sentence.
"Root" / "Origin" Explains the classical Latin/Greek etymology and word family roots.
"Pause" / "Resume" Temporarily pauses or resumes your commute audio session.

Siri Integration

You can launch your commute study session hands-free before driving by saying:

"Hey Siri, start Hands-Free Vocab" or "Hey Siri, open Hands-Free Vocab"
